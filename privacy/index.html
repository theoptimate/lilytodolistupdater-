Privacy and cookies

What this site collects, what advertising does with it, and how to turn it off.

Last updated: 29 August 2026

Before you publish this page: it is a working template written to match how the site is actually built, not legal advice. Have it reviewed against the law where your readers are, and update it whenever you add a vendor.

The short version

We do not ask you for an account, a name or an email to read anything. We do not sell personal information. The one thing that does watch you here is advertising, and you can control it — the links are below.

What we collect ourselves

Nothing that identifies you. There is no login, no comment system and no tracking script of ours on any page. Our host records standard server logs (IP address, timestamp, page requested, user agent) for security and to keep the site running; those are kept briefly and are not combined with anything else.

If you email us, we hold that email so we can reply to it.

Advertising

This site is funded by advertising served through Google AdSense. To do that, Google and its partners set cookies and read device identifiers in your browser. In Google's terms:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info or Your Online Choices in the EU.

Opting out does not remove advertising. It makes what you see less targeted.

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland

Personalised advertising in these regions requires your consent, gathered through a consent-management platform certified by Google. When you first arrive you will be asked whether you accept advertising and measurement cookies, and you can change that answer at any time from the same control. Declining does not restrict any content on this site.

If you are in California

We do not sell or share personal information in the sense the CCPA/CPRA uses, and we do not knowingly process the personal information of anyone under 16. Advertising vendors may treat ad identifiers as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising; the opt-out links above apply, and this site honours the Global Privacy Control signal where the ad stack supports it.

Analytics

We currently run no analytics product. If that changes, this page will name the product and say what it collects before it goes live.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

Material changes will be reflected in the date at the top of this page. If a change affects what is collected about you, it will be flagged on the site rather than made quietly.
