Seven Years at Half-Staff

Five days a year are written into the Flag Code. Every other day the flag comes down is a decision someone made — after a death, a shooting, a disaster. Between 2019 and 2025 that happened on 281 days, about eleven percent of all days, and the count swings from 61 in 2021 to 19 in 2024.

Each year is drawn as a barcode of 365 days. Hover any day to see the order behind it.

How a graphic gets made

Three steps, in this order, every time.

First — the primary record

Proclamations, filings, roll calls, disaster declarations. Never a number lifted from another article. If the primary source is unreachable, the graphic says so on its face.

Then — count it in the open

The counting rule is written down before the chart is drawn: what one unit is, what overlaps, what is excluded. It ships with the graphic so you can disagree with it precisely.

Last — publish the data too

Every figure on the page is computed from a dataset you can read in the page source. Correct one entry and the whole graphic updates. Nothing is typed in by hand.
